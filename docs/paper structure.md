Suggestions on how to write the paper *

The paper will have the following structure: Title page, Contents, Introduction, Chapters in which the method of solving the proposed problem is gradually presented, Conclusions, Bibliography and Appendices. Optionally, the list of figures and the list of tables in the document can be included. 

The paper is edited following the following recommendations:
for editing, diacritics are used.

Suggestions regarding the structure of the paper

1. Introduction

- 2-3 pages
- a brief description of the problem 
- description of the objectives of the paper
- possible advantages
- importance and topicality
- a brief presentation of each chapter

[The titles of chapters 2-5 are indicative; the emphasis is on the presentation of the content]

2. The problem addressed by the paper [the title of the chapter is dependent on the title of the paper] – (Domain analysis)

10 - 15 pages;
For example. for a paper entitled Automatic system for checking plagiarism at the source code level, this chapter can be called Plagiarism at the source code level;
describe in detail the problem that the work addresses;
presents the motivation on the basis of which the theme was chosen;
presents the need for an IT solution/mathematical model/economic analysis as a solution to the presented problem;
justify the actuality and importance of the problem;


Use of references in the text

For ideas, descriptions of technologies/solutions/theoretical or practical concepts taken from other sources, their source must be indicated by using [reference number from the bibliography]

For example, if they describe a problem related to IT Security, the text would be

In May 2017 saw one of the most extensive computer attacks, WannaCry [1], which was based on infecting a large number of Windows machines to propagate a ransomware virus [2].

And in the bibliography I would have 2 entries 
[1] Wikipedia, WannaCry ransomware attack, [Online], available at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WannaCry_ransomware_attack
[2] A. Young, M. Yung, Malicious Cryptography: Exposing Cryptovirology, Wiley. ISBN 0-7645-4975-8, 2004.

3. Methods used/Algorithms/Cybernetic models

- 6 – 10 pages
- the_technologies_used are briefly described
 - the algorithms, methodologies and models used are described; (for example, for an IT solution that solves an optimization problem or a transport problem, the methods used are described)

4. Solution architecture/Application design/Analysis design
- 6-10 pages
- the chapter must contain a component diagram or a diagram of the analysis stages;
- at least one flow chart for a functionality of the application;
- the functions of the application and the main modules are described;
- if the solution uses a database, describe its structure and the role of the tables;
- the application is described without referring to certain technologies;
5. Solution Implementation 

- 10 – 15 pages
- the specific elements of the bachelor's thesis are detailed, taking into account the coordinator's recommendations;
- the technologies used are briefly described with an emphasis on implementation
- the chapter contains use cases for the main functions of the application (with screenshots)
- describes how to implement the most interesting functions;
- describes the main implemented classes;

6. Conclusions

- 1-2 pages;
This chapter must include:
- conclusions regarding the degree of completion of the proposed solution;
- possible future developments of the solution;
- possible problems that require future research (perhaps at the master's);
- the advantages of the proposed solution;
- possible disadvantages;
